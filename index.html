The process

Four steps, no clean-up.

Babysafe replaces messy ink pads with a sealed, single-use wipe and sensitised paper — designed for a ward that doesn't have twenty minutes to spare.

1

Open the wipe

Tear open one individually sealed imprint wipe and wipe the infant's foot or hand. It will feel dry to the touch — that's normal, and part of what keeps it mess-free.

2

Press onto the card

Gently but firmly press the foot or hand onto the Babysafe identification card, sensitised certificate, or label — whichever the hospital is using.

3

Repeat for the other side

One wipe, one print. Repeat the same motion for the second foot or hand.

4

The print develops itself

No blotting, no ink on the baby, no ink on staff. The print appears on its own.

≈ 10–20 seconds · permanent · non-toxic · smudge-proof
